# BGET Rules & Governance

The short version lives in `#rules`. This is the full operating document.

---

## 1. The rules (short form — post in `#rules`)

> **BGET — five rules**
>
> 1. **Be kind.** No cruelty, no humiliation, no harassment. Disagree with ideas, not people.
> 2. **Be honest.** No deception, no impersonation, no misrepresentation of your work.
> 3. **Be useful.** Contribute more than you extract. Introduce yourself. Help someone weekly.
> 4. **Be humble.** No résumé spam, no unsolicited self-promo, no gatekeeping.
> 5. **Be responsible.** What you do here reflects on everyone. Our actions matter
>    even when nobody is watching.
>
> **Also:** no illegal activity, no doxxing, no sharing others' private messages,
> no recruiting members for outside ventures without their consent, no NSFW content,
> no spam or mass-DMs.
>
> Breaking rule 1, 2, or 5 is never a "joke gone wrong" defence.

## 2. Roles & responsibilities

| Role | Count | Responsibilities |
|------|-------|------------------|
| **Founder & Steward** | 1 | Protect culture, set standards, connect people, build alongside everyone, listen. **Does not win arguments by rank.** |
| **Stewards** | 2–4 | Moderation, onboarding oversight, event continuity, charter upkeep |
| **Guides** | rotates | Host events, mentor new members, lead challenges |
| **Members** | all | Contribute, help, follow the five rules |

**Founder's stated role:** *Founder & Steward — I protect the culture, connect
people, create opportunities, build alongside you, and listen.* Put that exact
sentence in `#welcome`. It sets the frame that BGET is not a hierarchy with you
at the top.

**Succession:** the charter includes a clause that the Founder can be
outvoted by Stewards on community matters, and that Stewards are appointed by
demonstrated contribution — not by friendship.

## 3. Moderation ladder

Escalate proportionally. Always prefer the lowest rung that works.

| Rung | Action | When |
|------|--------|------|
| 0 | **Ignore / don't feed** | Trolls, bad-faith, drive-by |
| 1 | **Private nudge (DM)** | First mistake, looks unintentional |
| 2 | **Public gentle redirect** | Behaviour needs a visible norm |
| 3 | **Formal warning** (logged) | Repeated after nudging, or clearly deliberate |
| 4 | **Timeout 24–72h** | Escalation, hostility, spam |
| 5 | **Removal** | Slurs, harassment, doxxing, scamming, repeated after warning |
| 6 | **Ban + note** | Illegal activity, predatory behaviour, malicious leaks |

**Standing rules for moderators:**

- **Never moderate while angry.** Wait 10 minutes. Or hand it to another mod.
- **Mod actions happen in DM first, public only if it's a pattern** others need to see addressed.
- **Log every rung-3+ action** in a private mod channel: who, what, when, outcome.
- **Two-mod rule for anything at rung 5+.** Nobody gets removed on one person's judgment.
- **The founder is not exempt.** If you break the rules, a Steward handles it. Say so publicly when it happens — it's the most trust-building thing you can do.
- **Appeals:** every removed member may appeal once, in writing, to a Steward
  who was not involved in the decision. Answer within 7 days either way.

## 4. Conflict resolution

1. **Assume good intent once.** Then deal with behaviour.
2. **Take it to voice.** 90% of community conflicts are text-misread.
3. **If unresolved, two Stewards mediate** with both parties present.
4. **If it concerns the Founder,** Stewards deliberate without the Founder in the room.
5. **Document the outcome** (one paragraph, private). Patterns matter more than incidents.

**Public drama policy:** disagreements in public channels are fine and healthy.
Coordinated pile-ons, subtweeting in other servers, and screenshot campaigns are not.

## 5. Contribution standards

**For discussions**
- Argue the claim, not the person.
- Steelman before you rebut.
- Cite when it matters; admit uncertainty when it doesn't.
- Changing your mind publicly is a *status upgrade* here, not a loss.

**For projects (BGET Labs)**
- Every project has: a one-line purpose, a named lead, a public board, and a
  definition of done.
- A project with no activity for 60 days gets archived — no shame, explicitly celebrated
  as "closed honestly".
- Contributors are credited. **Lead credit follows work, not titles.**
- Problem originators are credited too — discovering a problem is real work.
  Full credit system: [PEOPLE.md](PEOPLE.md).
- No project may use BGET's name, logo, or member list without Steward approval.

**For self-promotion**
- Allowed in `#showcase` and `#opportunities`, when it's genuinely useful to others.
- Not allowed as unsolicited DMs to members. **This is a hard rule** — DM-recruiting
  members for your venture without consent results in removal.

**For IP and openness**
- Members own what they make. Default is: personal projects stay personal.
- BGET-originated open-source projects use an OSI-approved licence (MIT/Apache-2.0/AGPL
  as appropriate) decided *before* the first commit.
- Anything commercial that grows out of a BGET project is discussed openly with
  its contributors first. See [SUSTAINABILITY.md](SUSTAINABILITY.md).

## 6. The charter

- Lives in `#manifesto` + this file. Version-controlled (a git repo is ideal).
- **Reviewed quarterly** with member proposals in a public thread.
- Changes need: a written proposal, 7 days of discussion, and majority of
  Stewards + no unresolved objection from Founder.
- Members may propose removing a Steward by a ⅔ Steward vote after a hearing.

**Why this matters:** you said BGET should belong to the community. A written,
amendable charter is the difference between a promise and a structure.

---

## 7. Safety & privacy

- No member's contact details, employer, location, or screenshots are shared outside BGET without consent.
- **Problem submissions** carry a visibility flag chosen by the submitter
  (public / BGET review only / private — see [PROBLEM-COMMONS.md](PROBLEM-COMMONS.md) §9).
  Nothing marked private is published, quoted, indexed, or shared. Personal
  details inside submissions follow the same rules as member data above.
- No DM logs are shared in public channels, including in moderation disputes.
- Minors are protected: no private 1:1 DMs between adults and under-18 members
  without a parent/guardian informed or a Steward present in the thread.
- Report path: DM any `@Steward`. Reports about a Steward go to another Steward;
  reports about the Founder go to all Stewards jointly.
