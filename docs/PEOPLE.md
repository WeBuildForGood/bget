# BGET People — visibility, credit, and the people building it

> **The founder starts the story. The contributors continue it.
> The impact proves it.**

---

## 1. The positioning line

> **BGET — a global network for turning meaningful problems into collective
> action. Founded by Nikhil Nagpure. Built with everyone who contributes.**

One sentence, both truths: a real person had the courage to start this, and it
does not belong to him. Use it in READMEs, profiles, and partner outreach
([BRANDING.md](BRANDING.md)). It doesn't replace the tagline — it answers
*"what is this?"* for someone who has never heard of BGET.

---

## 2. The founder: be visible, don't build a shrine

People are asked to trust BGET with their time, ideas, and sometimes sensitive
problems. *"Who is behind this?"* needs an answer — anonymity reads as evasion.

**Title: Founder & Steward.** Never CEO. The term and the exact sentence that
goes in `#welcome` are already fixed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) §2:

> *Founder & Steward — I protect the culture, connect people, create
> opportunities, build alongside you, and listen.*

**The founder page holds:**

- A photo — authentic, not corporate. **Not in the site hero; the mission is the hero.**
- **Why I started BGET** — a real letter: the frustration, the belief, and
  crucially *what BGET is not*
- What I believe — 3–5 principles
- Background, selected work, GitHub/links — **evidence, not adjectives**
- *"BGET started with one person. It isn't meant to remain one person's work."*

**Rules:**

- The founder page is **shorter** than the community story. Founder establishes
  trust; community establishes legitimacy; problems establish purpose; Labs
  establish activity; impact establishes results. In that order.
- Never "created by a visionary..." — people smell self-promotion instantly.
  Show work; let visitors conclude "this person is serious."
- Nobody's contribution ranks higher because they founded it. The Founder can
  be outvoted by Stewards (GOVERNANCE §2) — say so; it's trust-building.

---

## 3. Never invent a team

Fake titles destroy credibility faster than having no team page.

| Reality | Show |
|---------|------|
| Just you | Founder & Steward, and *"Contributors: growing"* |
| 1–10 people | Founder & Steward + real contributors with real roles |
| 10–50 | Add Stewards, Guides, Problem Stewards, Lab leads — all earned |

No CTO. No Chief Innovation Officer. No advisors who haven't advised. Every
person shown exists and does the thing. An honest "nobody yet" beats a
grid of stock smiles.

## 5. Contribution is bigger than code

Recognition categories — a project lists the ones that apply:

Problem discovery · Local knowledge · Research · Engineering · Science · Design ·
Data · Field work · Writing · Translation · Testing · Documentation ·
Project management · Community building · Partnerships · Fundraising ·
Deployment · Evaluation

Someone who interviews affected people, translates a document, finds the
research, photographs field conditions, or introduces the right official is a
contributor. Full stop.

**Local knowledge is a discipline.** Someone living a problem outranks ten
experts who aren't. It carries equal weight in credit and a seat at framing
(PROBLEM-COMMONS §8) — not a footnote.

## 6. Credit rules

From [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) §5: **Lead credit follows work, not titles.**
The rest of the system:

1. **No project ever says only "BGET solved this."** The record names people:
   problem identified by · steward · research · engineering · field testing ·
   documentation.
2. **The originator is named** — with consent — *"submitted by Priya,
   Maharashtra"*, never *"User #4839."* Discovering a problem is real work.
3. **A contribution ledger per project**, dated and permanent — a record, not a
   scoreboard:

   | Person | Contribution | Date |
   |--------|--------------|------|
   | A | Problem discovery | Jan 2027 |
   | B | Research | Jan 2027 |

4. **No XP, points, streaks, or leaderboards.** They reward volume; BGET
   rewards quality ([DISCORD.md](DISCORD.md) §3 — same instinct, applied to credit).
5. **Nothing published without consent.** Contributors choose: public profile /
   name only / anonymous. Photo, location, employer, links — all opt-in.
   Fits [VALUES.md](VALUES.md) §8 and [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) §7.

## 7. Contributor profiles (later)

When they exist: `people/<name>` — name, location, discipline, short bio,
problems worked on, Labs, consented recognition, links. A portable record of
meaningful work: *"BGET contributor — Clean Water Initiative"* on a CV beats a
Discord role.

This is Phase D in [PROBLEM-COMMONS.md](PROBLEM-COMMONS.md) §12. Don't build it
before framing and credit work without a website.

---

## 8. What we don't do

- Invent people, titles, or numbers.
- Founder worship. The face appears; the mission stays the hero.
- Make Founder the highest status. Starting BGET is not a permanent
  contribution ranking.
- Style contributors as "random users" next to a founder-shaped executive.
  Roles differ; human value doesn't.

---

## 9. The rule, in one line

> BGET was started by one person — and the purpose of BGET is to eventually
> make the founder **less** central, not more central.
