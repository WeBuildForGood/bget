# BGET Values — in behavioural terms

A value nobody can act on is decoration. Each value below is written as
**the behaviour we reward** and **the behaviour we correct.**

---

## 1. Character before capability

**We reward:** people who keep promises, admit mistakes early, and do the right
thing when it costs them something.
**We correct:** brilliance used as a licence. Status is never a shield.

## 2. Intellectual humility

**We reward:** "I don't know", "I was wrong", "can you explain that to me",
"here's something I'm struggling with".
**We correct:** performing expertise, dismissing questions, one-upmanship,
citing credentials instead of reasoning.

## 3. Constructive disagreement

**We reward:** attacking the idea, steelmanning the other side, changing your
mind publicly when the evidence warrants it.
**We correct:** personal attacks, sarcasm as a weapon, pile-ons, arguing to win.

## 4. Generosity as a default

**We reward:** answering questions, reviewing someone's code, making an
introduction, sharing a resource without being asked, crediting others.
**We correct:** hoarding knowledge, gatekeeping, only showing up to ask for something.

## 5. Follow-through

**We reward:** shipping, small consistent contributions, closing the loop,
saying no rather than silently ghosting.
**We correct:** enthusiasm followed by disappearance, starting everything and finishing nothing,
taking on roles you never actually do.

## 6. Respect for people and planet

**We reward:** kindness to newcomers, patience with different skill levels,
environmental and social awareness in what we build.
**We correct:** cruelty disguised as "just being direct", exploiting people,
building harm and calling it progress.

## 7. Ownership without empire-building

**We reward:** "I'll take care of that", then actually doing it.
**We correct:** seeking authority without service, treating members as an audience
or a workforce.

## 8. Privacy and trust

**We reward:** keeping confidences, asking before quoting someone externally,
handling personal data carefully.
**We correct:** screenshotting drama, sharing member details, recruiting people
from BGET for outside ventures without consent.

---

## The two-sentence culture test

Before posting, before moderating, before recruiting, ask:

1. **Would I be comfortable if this were screenshotted and shown to everyone here?**
2. **Does this make BGET more or less the place exceptional people want to stay?**

If either answer is no — don't send it.

---

## Culture is set by the first ten

Values are not enforced by documents. They are enforced by **who you let in,
what you tolerate on day one, and what you celebrate.**

- What you **tolerate** becomes the standard.
- What you **celebrate** becomes the goal.
- What you **ignore** becomes the culture.

Write that on the wall of the mod team.
