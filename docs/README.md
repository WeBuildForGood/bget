# BGET — Build. Grow. Evolve. Together.

**The operating system for the BGET community.**

This folder contains everything needed to run BGET: its identity, culture,
membership filter, Discord structure, activities, the Problem Commons (how
problems enter and become work), the people architecture, growth plan,
governance, and a 90-day execution plan.

## The one-line summary

> Build boldly. Grow continuously. Evolve consciously. Help others rise.
> Leave the world better than you found it.

## The two-line summary (what BGET does)

> Bring us a problem.
> We'll bring together the people who can help.

## Three audiences, three doors

| If you arrive with... | You are | Start here |
|-----------------------|---------|------------|
| **A problem** | Someone carrying a real-world problem | [PROBLEM-COMMONS.md](PROBLEM-COMMONS.md) → [templates/problem-submission.md](templates/problem-submission.md) — open to anyone, no membership needed |
| **Skills** | Someone who wants to help | [MEMBERSHIP.md](MEMBERSHIP.md) (selective) or join one Lab directly |
| **Resources** | An organisation: funding, data, facilities, expertise | [SUSTAINABILITY.md](SUSTAINABILITY.md) — partner, sponsor, or field-deploy |

The public copy mirrors this: *I have a problem · I want to help · We have
resources.* Every visitor should find their door in one screen.

## Non-negotiable principle

**Quality before quantity.** BGET grows by becoming a place exceptional people want to stay —
not by collecting members. Culture produces growth; growth does not produce culture.
