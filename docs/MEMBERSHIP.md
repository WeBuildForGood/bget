# BGET Membership — the filter

> "You don't attract exceptional people by asking for applications.
> You attract them by being a place exceptional people want to stay."

The application exists to **protect the existing members**, not to gatekeep status.

---

## 1. The twelve dimensions

Score each 1–5. **Character is a gate, not a weighted average.**

| # | Dimension | What a 5 looks like | What a 1 looks like |
|---|-----------|--------------------|--------------------|
| 1 | **Character** (GATE) | Keeps promises, honest when costly | Manipulative, dishonest, self-serving |
| 2 | Kindness | Makes people feel respected and safe | Treats people as tools or audiences |
| 3 | Curiosity | Asks better questions than they answer | Wants to appear knowledgeable |
| 4 | Ambition | Real goals, visible consistent effort | Talk without work |
| 5 | Competence | Can show something built/done | No evidence of doing anything |
| 6 | Humility | "I don't know", accepts correction | Defensiveness, credential-flashing |
| 7 | Critical thinking | Questions own assumptions | Assumption-repeating, tribal |
| 8 | Collaboration | Makes others better | Extracts, competes destructively |
| 9 | Responsibility | Thinks about consequences | Blames, cuts corners |
| 10 | Discipline | Follows through repeatedly | Starts, never finishes |
| 11 | Growth mindset | Uses criticism as fuel | Fragile, defensive, hostile to feedback |
| 12 | Positive intent | Wants capability to create | Wants capability to dominate |

**Admit rule:** Character ≥ 4 AND average of remaining ≥ 3.0.
Anyone scoring 1–2 on Character is declined regardless of every other score.

## 2. Application questions

Copy from [templates/application.md](templates/application.md).

Design notes:

- **No CV, no certifications, no "years of experience" field.** Deliberately.
  This signals immediately that BGET is not a résumé club.
- Ask for **one thing they're struggling with.** Genuine answers reveal more
  than any achievement list.
- Ask **what they've made.** Links welcome, not required. A small honest project
  beats three impressive-sounding claims.
- Keep it under **10 minutes.** Long applications select for persistence, not quality.

---

## 3. The conversation (15–20 minutes, voice)

Every applicant gets one short voice conversation. Not an interrogation —
a mutual evaluation. They are also deciding whether *we* are worth their time.

**Questions that reveal character:**

1. "What's something you believed two years ago that you've since changed your mind about?"
2. "Tell me about a time you failed or were wrong. What happened?"
3. "What are you working on right now, and what's genuinely hard about it?"
4. "What can you do for other BGET members that most people can't?"
5. "Who in your life pushes you to be better?"
6. "What would make you leave this community in six months?"

**Green flags:** specific answers, self-awareness, curiosity about BGET itself,
asking real questions, comfortable saying "I don't know".

**Red flags:** only talking about achievements, vague grandiosity, badmouthing
previous communities or employers, no questions for you, immediately asking
"what's in it for me", treating the call as a formality.

**Never advance someone who:** interrupts constantly, derides people with less
experience, or cannot name a single thing they're bad at.

## 4. The trial period (first 30 days)

Membership is **provisional for 30 days.** New members get full read access and
can post, but are not eligible for team leads or moderator consideration.

**Confirm when they:**

- [ ] Introduced themselves in `#introductions`
- [ ] Participated in ≥ 2 threads meaningfully (not just "nice!")
- [ ] Helped someone else at least once
- [ ] Attended ≥ 1 event, OR contributed to ≥ 1 open thread asynchronously
- [ ] Demonstrated no conduct issues

**End when they:** spam, self-promote repeatedly after a warning, are abusive,
or are simply invisible for 30 days with no response to outreach (they can
re-apply in 90 days — no shame in that).

## 5. Sourcing: how to find the first 10

Do **not** post publicly. Recruit personally, from people you have actually
interacted with.

**Where exceptional early members come from:**

- People you've collaborated with (work, open source, college, side projects)
- Thoughtful commenters you've seen in communities you frequent
- People who helped you when you didn't ask for anything in return
- Members referred by confirmed members (after member #10)

**The invite rule:** you should be able to complete this sentence honestly —
*"I've seen this person handle X, and they handled it well."*
If you can't, you're recruiting a stranger's potential, not a known quantity.

Use [templates/invite-message.md](templates/invite-message.md).

**Referrals:** every confirmed member gets **one referral slot per quarter.**
Referrals skip the public application but not the conversation or the trial.

**Day-to-day playbook:** sources, the stranger → member approach ladder,
outreach messages, and the weekly routine live in [RECRUITING.md](RECRUITING.md).

## 6. Stages of membership

| Stage | Size | Access |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Founding 10** | 1–10 | Full voice in direction, help shape channels/events/standards |
| **Circle 50** | 11–50 | Full membership + one referral slot; founding members become mentors |
| **Community 500** | 51–500 | Application + trial; teams and project leads emerge; moderation team forms |
| **Network 5,000** | 501–5,000 | Tiered access; strong automation; humans still review every application |

**What must change at each stage — see [GROWTH.md](GROWTH.md).**

## 7. What we tell people who don't get in

Reject warmly, personally, and without false hope:

> "Thanks for the honest answers — genuinely. BGET is small on purpose right now,
> and I'm only able to take in people I can actually spend time with. That's not
> a verdict on you. If you'd like, re-apply in 90 days — and in the meantime,
> here are two things I think you'd get a lot out of: [resource]."

No ghosting. No form-letter rejections. The person you decline today may be
someone you want back in six months — or someone who tells others that BGET
treats people well, which is the cheapest marketing that exists.

---

## 8. Open to problems, selective with people

BGET runs three doors with three different standards:

| Door | Standard | Why |
|------|----------|-----|
| **Submit a problem** ([PROBLEM-COMMONS.md](PROBLEM-COMMONS.md)) | Open to anyone, anywhere | The world doesn't need our permission to have problems |
| **Join BGET** (sections 1–7 above) | Application → conversation → 30-day trial | The filter protects members from extractive people |
| **Join a Lab** | Depends on what the project needs | Some work needs a domain expert; some needs a translator |

This is not a contradiction, and the public copy should say so plainly —
*"Why membership is selective."* The filter was written to keep **people** out
who would make the community worse. It was never meant to filter **problems** —
especially the ones from people who have no community to ask in the first
place.
