# BGET Activities — the programming

> A manifesto doesn't make people stay. **Something happening does.**

Five recurring formats. Run them like rituals, not one-offs. Consistency is
what turns a chat server into a community.

Run sheets: [templates/event-kit.md](templates/event-kit.md)

---

## The core five

### 1. 🔨 BGET Build Night — *weekly, 90 min, voice + screen share*

> "We're building something together tonight."

- **Format:** 10 min kickoff → 60 min silent/mic-open building together →
  20 min show-and-tell in `#showcase`.
- **Scope:** anything. Automation, a website, a CLI tool, a cyber lab, an AI
  experiment, a hardware prototype, an NGO website.
- **Rule:** no spectators. Everyone present is building *something*, even if it's
  a README.
- **Why weekly:** it's the heartbeat. One missed week is fine; two is a slump;
  three and people forget you exist.

### 2. 🎙️ BGET Deep Dive — *weekly or fortnightly, 20–30 min*

> One member presents something they're obsessed with.

- **Examples:** "How LLM inference actually works" · "Why cryptography ≠ security"
  · "Can we build decentralized energy monitoring?" · "What I learned failing my first project"
- **Format:** 25 min talk → 15 min genuine Q&A. Recorded, notes posted to `#resources`.
- **Who speaks:** anyone, including week-one members. Lower the barrier: a
  "Deep Dive Lite" (10 min, one idea, no slides) for first-timers.
- **Rotating host.** You should not be the permanent speaker — you're the convener.

### 3. 🧩 BGET Challenge — *fortnightly, 1 week, teams of 2–4*

> A small collaborative problem with a deadline.

- **Format:** announced Monday in `#projects` → teams form → one week →
  demos Sunday → judged on *usefulness + effort + collaboration*, not polish.
- **Sample prompts:**
  - Build something that reduces wasted electricity.
  - A tool that makes donating to a local NGO 10× easier.
  - A privacy-first study group matcher for students.
  - Visualize this public dataset in a way a non-technical person understands.
- **Prize:** recognition + a slot to present at the next Deep Dive. Cash only
  when you have sponsors (see [SUSTAINABILITY.md](SUSTAINABILITY.md)).

### 4. 🧠 BGET Think — *weekly, async-first, in `#deep-discussions`*

> A question worth arguing about well.

- **Prompt posted Monday.** Discussion runs all week, async so time zones don't kill it.
- **Format:** someone is assigned "steelman moderator" — they summarise the best
  opposing arguments before the thread closes. Post the summary Friday.
- **Sample prompts:**
  - Does technological progress necessarily improve human life?
  - Is open source sustainable, or is it unpaid labour with extra steps?
  - Should a community select for skill, character, or neither?
  - Can ambition be peaceful?
- **Rule:** the objective is thinking better together, **not winning.**
  Personal attacks = mod action, immediately.

### 5. 🌍 BGET Impact — *monthly, physical or measurable*

> Make the philosophy real. Measured, reported, photographed.

- **Forms:** tree planting · local cleanup · free programming teaching ·
  building software for an NGO · donating technical work · open-source maintenance
- **The measure matters.** "We planted 40 trees with 9 people" beats "we did good".
  Post numbers in `#impact-reports`.
- **Why monthly:** this is where "positive influence on Earth and nature" stops
  being a slogan. It's also the most shareable thing BGET produces.

## Quarterly & milestone events

| Event | Cadence | Purpose |
|-------|---------|---------|
| **BGET Showcase** | Quarterly | Every active project demos. The external-facing moment. |
| **BGET Retreat / Meetup** | 2×/year (as soon as feasible) | Relationships compound in person faster than in any channel. |
| **Charter Review** | Quarterly | Members propose changes to rules/governance. Keeps it *theirs*. |
| **Project Retros** | End of each challenge/project | What worked, what didn't, who contributed. |
| **New Member Cohort** | Monthly | Admit and onboard in small batches, not drip-fed. |

## Minimum viable programming (weeks 1–8)

Don't launch all five at once. You'll burn out and cancel, which teaches people
your events are optional.

1. **Week 1:** Build Night (weekly, non-negotiable)
2. **Week 3:** add `#deep-discussions` Think prompt (async, near-zero cost)
3. **Week 5:** add first Deep Dive (10-min lite version, you host)
4. **Week 7:** add first Challenge
5. **Week 9:** first Impact day
6. From week 9: the full rhythm.

**If you can only keep one alive: keep Build Night.** Shared work builds
relationships faster than any amount of conversation.

## What kills communities (guard against these)

- **Zero unanswered introductions.** Every intro gets a human reply in 24h.
- **Events announced once and never repeated.** Rituals, not announcements.
- **Only the founder talks.** Assign hosts by week 4.
- **Discussion without doing.** Balance THINK and BUILD roughly 1:1.
- **Announcing big plans instead of shipping small ones.** Cancel culture loves
  announcements; members love evidence.
