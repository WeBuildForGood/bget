# The BGET Manifesto

*This is the public-facing text. Post it in `#manifesto`, put it on the website, pin it.*

---

## BGET — Build. Grow. Evolve. Together.

We are a community of young, ambitious, capable people who believe that
**capability without character is not enough.**

Anyone can learn to code. Fewer learn to think clearly. Fewer still combine
skill with humility, ambition with kindness, and ambition with responsibility.

BGET exists for those people.

### What we do

**🛠️ Build.** Software, startups, research, open source, businesses, hardware,
creative work — real things that solve real problems.

**🌱 Grow.** Technically, intellectually, physically, emotionally, socially.
We do not separate the engineer from the human.

**🧠 Evolve.** We challenge our own assumptions, receive criticism without
fragility, and become better thinkers and leaders than we were last month.

**🤝 Together.** We collaborate instead of competing destructively. Someone
else's win is information we can use, not a threat to survive.

**🌍 Impact.** We measure success by what leaves the world better than we
found it — people, society, and nature.

**☮️ Harmoniously.** Ambition without cruelty. Competition without hatred.
Disagreement without disrespect.

### What we believe

- **Our actions matter, even when nobody is watching.** Integrity is not a
  performance. It is what you do in the dark.
- **Intellectual humility and high ambition belong together.** "I don't know —
  let's figure it out" is the most powerful sentence in any room.
- **Character is a prerequisite, not a bonus.** A brilliant person who
  manipulates, humiliates, or exploits others does not belong here.
- **We are not a résumé club.** We would rather ask for help than perform
  expertise. Struggling out loud is respected here; pretending not to struggle is not.
- **The first ten set the tone for the next thousand.** We would rather stay
  small and excellent than grow fast and become ordinary.

### What we are not

- Not a networking farm.
- Not a self-promotion channel.
- Not a place where status is measured in certifications.
- Not owned by any founder's startup. **BGET belongs to its members.**
- Not for people who are only here to extract.

### The BGET standard

We evaluate ourselves and each other on twelve dimensions:

| | |
|---|---|
| Character | Honest, trustworthy, keeps promises |
| Kindness | Does not treat people as tools |
| Curiosity | Wants to understand, not merely to appear knowledgeable |
| Ambition | Has meaningful goals and actually works on them |
| Competence | Can show something they have built or done |
| Humility | Comfortable saying "I don't know" |
| Critical thinking | Questions assumptions — including their own |
| Collaboration | Makes other people better |
| Responsibility | Thinks about consequences before acting |
| Discipline | Follows through |
| Growth mindset | Takes criticism without becoming defensive |
| Positive intent | Wants their capability to create, not destroy |

Character is the gate. Everything else is built on top of it.

### Our commitment to each other

If you join BGET, we promise you:

1. **You will be surrounded by people who make you better.**
2. **You will be judged by your conduct, not your credentials.**
3. **You will be helped when you ask.**
4. **You will be told the truth, kindly.**
5. **Your work and your words are safe here.**

And we ask three things of you:

1. **Show up.** Contribute more than you extract.
2. **Stay humble.** Nobody here is finished.
3. **Leave it better.** Every channel, every project, every relationship you touch.

---

**Build boldly. Grow continuously. Evolve consciously.
Help others rise. Leave the world better than you found it.**

— *BGET*
