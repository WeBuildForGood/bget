# BGET Branding — name, identity, voice

---

## The name

**BGET** — pronounced "bee-get".

**Meaning:** **B**uild · **G**row · **E**volve · **T**ogether.

It's short, typeable, unusual enough to be ownable, and it already carries its
own definition. That's a genuinely good foundation — don't over-engineer it.

**Domain/open-source handle check before you commit anywhere:**
`bget` is short enough that handles may be taken. Search and secure early:
domain (`bget.org` / `bget.io` / `bget.dev`), GitHub org, Discord vanity URL,
and the main socials — *even if you don't use them yet.* Squatting on your own
name is cheaper than rebranding later.

## Tagline options

| Tagline | Use when |
|---------|----------|
| **Build. Grow. Evolve. Together.** | Primary — it *is* the name. Always works. |
| *Where capability meets character.* | Secondary — for the membership/recruitment page |
| *Leave it better than you found it.* | Culture & Impact messaging |
| *High ambition. Low ego.* | Short, punchy, for social bios |
| **Bring us a problem. We'll bring the people who can help.** | Problem Commons — the second line under the hero, above the two CTAs |

**Recommendation:** primary = *Build. Grow. Evolve. Together.*
Secondary = *Where capability meets character.* — that second one is your
differentiator. It says exactly what you told me you're looking for.

**Positioning line** (for READMEs, profiles, partner outreach — where BGET
needs explaining in one sentence; see [PEOPLE.md](PEOPLE.md) §1):

> A global network for turning meaningful problems into collective action.
> Founded by Nikhil Nagpure. Built with everyone who contributes.

It doesn't replace the tagline; it answers *"what is this?"* for someone who
has never heard of BGET.

## Identity (pragmatic, no designer required)

**Colours**

| Role | Hex | Note |
|------|-----|------|
| Primary — deep green | `#1F7A4D` | Growth, nature, calm ambition |
| Secondary — warm gold | `#E8B84B` | The "Steward" role, accents, energy |
| Ink | `#101512` | Text |
| Paper | `#F7F5F0` | Backgrounds — warm off-white, not sterile white |
| Accent (sparingly) | `#2E5EAA` | Links, CTAs |

Green + gold is a strong, uncommon pairing and it maps directly to your mission
(growth/Earth) without being "another purple SaaS gradient".

**Typography:** one display font (e.g. **Sora**, **Space Grotesk**, or
**Bricolage Grotesque**) + one text font (e.g. **Inter**). Two fonts total.

**Logo direction:** don't commission one yet. Start with a **wordmark** —
"BGET" set in the display font, tight tracking, gold period or underscore.
Wordmarks age well, cost nothing, and look intentional at any size. Commission
a real mark only when you have 50+ members who care enough to have an opinion.

**Imagery:** avoid stock photos of people high-fiving. Use:
- Real screenshots of what members built
- Impact-day photos (actual trees, actual cleanups)
- Simple geometric shapes in brand colours

## Voice & tone

BGET sounds like **a sharp, kind friend who takes you seriously.**

| We sound like | We never sound like |
|--------------|---------------------|
| Direct but warm | Corporate ("leverage synergies") |
| Curious | Preachy or motivational-poster |
| Confident and specific | Hype ("🚀 REVOLUTIONIZING TECH") |
| Self-aware, a bit dry | Try-hard, emoji-stuffed |
| Humble about what we don't know | Guru ("10x your life") |

**Writing rules:**
- Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No filler.
- Say "we" for culture, "you" for invitations, "I" only when the founder is personally speaking.
- Emoji: use them as *structure* (section markers, channel icons) — not as decoration in every line.
- **Never** claim moral superiority. BGET shows its values; it doesn't announce them at people.

**Anti-patterns to ban from all BGET copy:**
- "Join our exclusive community of like-minded individuals"
- "Networking opportunity"
- "We're building the future of X"
- "Elite"/"top-tier"/"10x" as self-description
- "Submit your problem and BGET will solve it" — promise an honest review, never an outcome
- Exclamation marks more than once per message

## Culture artifacts worth producing early

1. **A one-page visual manifesto** — the manifesto text, well-typeset, shareable as an image. Highest-leverage asset you can make in a weekend.
2. **A public `#showcase` feed** — the community's real portfolio. Better than any landing page.
3. **A quarterly Impact report** — numbers + photos. This is your most credible marketing.
4. **A "What BGET is not" section everywhere** — it filters people out pre-application, which is exactly what a filter should do.

## Naming conventions

- **Events:** `BGET Build Night`, `BGET Deep Dive`, `BGET Challenge`, `BGET Think`, `BGET Impact`, `BGET Showcase`
- **Projects:** `BGET Labs → <project name>` (project names stand alone; not everything needs "BGET" in front)
- **Channels:** lowercase-with-hyphens, short, no punctuation
- **Roles:** emoji + noun, no ranks beyond what [DISCORD.md](DISCORD.md) defines
- **Never** use "BGET" as a verb toward people ("go BGET it") — it will happen anyway; don't encourage it.
